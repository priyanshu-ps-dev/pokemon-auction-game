Auction Game Rules & Valuation Formula

Tournament Rules

  • Room Size: Up to 8 players (min 2 to start).
  • Starting Purse: Default 🪙 100,000 credits.
  • Team Capacity: Exactly 6 Pokémon per trainer.
  • Auction Countdown: 15 seconds per Pokémon.
  • Anti-Snipe: Every valid bid resets timer to 15s.
  • Reserve Budget Rule: You must retain enough funds to afford the minimum bid for every remaining empty slot.
  • Victory: Sum of Power Ratings of your 6 Pokémon!

Tie-Breaking Order

  1. Higher Combined Base Stat Total (BST).
  2. Higher Highest Individual Pokémon Power Rating.
  3. Higher Remaining Purse balance.
  4. Declared Tie.
